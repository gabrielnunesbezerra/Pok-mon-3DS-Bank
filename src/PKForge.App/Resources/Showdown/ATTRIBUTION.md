# Showdown sprite attribution

The front sprites in `front/` and `front-shiny/` and the box icon sheet `icons.png` come from
[Pokémon Showdown](https://pokemonshowdown.com) by [Smogon](https://www.smogon.com), and are
used with Smogon's permission for free, open-source software.

The Black/White-style sprites of later generations and the box icons are drawn by the
community artists of Smogon's sprite projects; see
[Showdown's credits](https://pokemonshowdown.com/credits) for the full list.

The files are fetched from Showdown and mapped to PKHeX forms ahead of time.
